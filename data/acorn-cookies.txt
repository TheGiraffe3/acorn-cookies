mission "Acorn Delights: Part 1"
	minor
	name "Acorns for Anoo"
	description "Bring Eeeya to <destination> to deliver <cargo> to her missing granddaughter."
	source "Allhome"
	destination "Stonebreak"
	passengers 1
	cargo "acorn cookies" 3
	to offer
		random < 35
		has "First Contact: Unfettered: offered"
	on offer
		conversation
			`As you pass by a kitchen supply store in the spaceport, a dishevelled elderly Hai looks towards you with a face of deep concern. She waves you down.`
			choice
				`	(See what she wants.)`
				`	(Ignore her.)`
					decline
			`	"My name is Eeeya, and I'm looking for my lost granddaughter, Anoo. She just passed the medical school, and I was going to give her three tons of cookies, but she's gone! Please help me find her." Her soft voice has a chittering edge much stronger than other Hai you've spoken with. She also hesitates before certain words, as if unsure which article to add, giving her speech a unusual rhythm.`
				goto "actual questions"
			label questions
			`	"So please, take me to find my granddaughter."`
			label "actual questions"
			choice
				`	"Three tons of cookies!?"`
					goto tons
				`	"She's gone?"`
					goto gone
				`	"Why don't you ask the authorities for help?"`
					goto authorities
				`	"Can't your friends help you find her?"`
					goto friends
				`	"Let's go find her!"`
					goto accept
				`	"I have other places to be."`
					decline
			label tons
			`	"Well, I... I don't know... I cook when I'm worried, and Anoo has been missing for three months! I've sealed all three tons of cookies. They should last a year or two. Maybe she'll share them with her friends? She has many friends! Tons of friends! They all miss her, but none are willing to help...`
				goto questions
			label gone
			`	"Anoo passed medical school on the Frostmark four months ago, near the top of her class! A famous hospital on the Hai-home needed a new trauma surgeon and said she would be perfect for the job. She accepted the position and said she would be on vacation for a few weeks.`
			`	"A month later, she told them she won't be coming, and nobody has heard from her since.`
				goto questions
			label authorities
			`	"I talked to the Central Medical Bureau, and they told me she got approval for practicing medicine anywhere in Hai space or beyond. They've heard nothing from her since she got approval. She has to register new medicines with the Bureau, so they should have heard from her.`
			`	"The police told me that several other people have reported Anoo missing, but they refused to tell me anything else. They said I had to talk to military, but they won't tell me anything.`
			`	"Maybe she's involved in something secret? Or illegal? No, she'd never do that. Where is she?`
				goto questions
			label friends
			`	"I have few friends, and none of them are interested in finding a missing Hai who could be anywhere. None of my customers want to help either, and her family is all dead.`
				goto questions
			label accept
			`	"She always talked about doing free work for those who couldn't afford medical treatment. When she was little, her mother would take her to Stonebreak to help at local shelters. Let's start there."`
				accept
	on visit
		dialog "You forgot Eeeya or some of her mountains of cookies. Maybe they're in one of your escorts?"

mission "Acorn Delights: Part 3"
	landing
	name "Acorns for Anoo"
	description "Bring Eeeya to <destination> to deliver <cargo> to her missing granddaughter."
	clearance `After hearing the name Eeeya, the Darkcloak flight controller excitedly responds, "Acorn Delights!" and you're granted landing clearance.`
	source "Cloudfire"
	destination "Darkcloak"
	passengers 1
	cargo "acorn cookies" 3
	to offer
		has "Acorn Delights: Part 2: done"
	on offer
		conversation
			`Eeeya takes you to the visitor's office and asks to see the Cloudfire military commander. Upon refusal, Eeeya throws a fit; after an hour of her panic they agree to let her speak with the commander.`
			`	After a few minutes, she comes back looking despondent, and you lead her back to <ship>.`
			`	"They said she doesn't work for them. I think they know where she is, but they won't tell me anything.`
			`	"There's one more place we can try, but I don't think you're going to like it. She may have joined Unfettered Hai."`
			label questions
			choice
				`	"Why didn't you mention this earlier?"`
					goto mention
				`	"Why would a doctor join a band of warmongers?"`
					goto warmonger
				`	"As the honorable Captain <first> <last>, I say let's go find her!"`
					goto accept
				`	"I'd be happy to help."`
					goto accept
				`	"Unfettered space is too dangerous for me. You will have to find another captain."`
					decline
			label mention
			`	"I was afraid it would scare you away. Humans are scared of our ships, and the Unfettered are deadly warriors. Besides, the other places were more likely. Many of her mother's family have joined the Unfettered in the past, but I didn't think that would happen to her."`
				goto questions
			label warmonger
			`	"Anoo was always sympathetic to the cause of the Unfettered Hai. Not their pointless war - she hated that - but she respected their drive to progress and their gift for change. Her father's family tried to stop her ideas, and we thought we succeeded, but... maybe...`
			`	"A trauma surgeon for the Unfettered Hai? I wonder if she planned this the years in advance?"`
				goto questions
			label accept
			`	"If Anoo went anywhere, it would be the Darkcloak. The largest Unfettered hospital is there. Many soldiers come back from raids injured on Darkcloak. A trauma surgeon would have a lot of work to do."`
				accept
	on visit
		dialog "You forgot Eeeya or some of her mountains of cookies. Maybe they're in one of your escorts?"

mission "Acorn Delights: Part 4"
	landing
	name "Acorn Delights"
	description "Take Eeeya to <destination> to finalize some paperwork regarding the Acorn Delights shop."
	source "Darkcloak"
	destination "Allhome"
	passengers 1
	to offer
		has "Acorn Delights: Part 3: done"
	on offer
		payment 3000
		log "Finally found Anoo. It turns out she joined the Unfettered. I am supposed to take Eeeya to <destination> now."
		conversation
			`You're ushered from the landing platform to a nearby hospital, where Eeeya finally manages to convey your intent. A few Unfettered Hai nearby are muttering in their language, and you keep hearing the Hai word for "cookies" as they watch Eeeya. Along the way, you notice an odd contrast of squalor and advanced machinery. Unlike their southern brethren, it seems the Unfettered value function and progress over aesthetics and comfort.`
			`	As you rise through the main tower of the hospital, you're surprised at how advanced it is. Despite their willingness to die in battle, the Unfettered seem to value the lives of the survivors. Perhaps this hospital is to heal them quickly so that they can fight again as soon as possible.`
			`	You reach a high floor filled with personal offices. Eeeya knocks on each door, apologizing when the Unfettered inside aren't who she's looking for, until you finally reach the very last room.`
			`	Eeeya opens this door, and runs to the Hai inside it - Anoo. They begin to speak in Hai, then remember that you are in the room.`
			`	"Please, Anoo, let's speak in the human language. <first> came all this way for me, and it's only fair that Captain be able to understand what's going on."`
			`	The conversation then turns to Anoo's new occupation.`
			`	"Why did you come here, Anoo? The Unfettered hate us."`
			`	"They don't hate us in particular, grandma. They hate what we've become: frivolous, uninventive, and self-important. Our species is fading into history through a slow death of mediocrity. Most Unfettered see warfare as our only salvation.`
			`	"We think of them as mindless brutes, but they're not! They have scientists, doctors, musicians, and writers. Our resources dwarf theirs, but they thrive with what little they have. Soon the Unfettered will have many more planets and almost unlimited resources. The era of scarcity will be over, and they'll have the power to choose their future.`
			`	"If enough good people join the Unfettered, we can change their minds and set them on a more productive path. They'll need good doctors."`
			`	"But-" Eeeya objects.`
			choice
				`	"Anoo is right; the Unfettered are a noble people. I would recommend joining them."`
				`	"There's no good reason to join the Unfettered. What have they ever done except kill innocent Hai?"`
					goto "don't join"
			label "join unfettered"
			`	"Well... I really don't know..."`
			choice
				`	"Never mind, you're right. Joining the Unfettered is a bad idea after all."`
				`	"I stand by their guns. The Unfettered are honorable."`
					goto "really join"
			label "don't join"
			`	"I'm sorry, Anoo, but I really think the Captain <first> is right. I'm not the kind of person to be friendly with a group who starves and yet paints such a romantic picture of itself. And in addition, I doubt there would be a way to support a family of two here; nuts by themselves are costly enough, let alone cookies. The hospital work here can only pay so much.`
			`	"Captain, could you take me back to the <planet> please? I'd like to sign the forms and give you some stock in my Acorn Delights factory. In the meantime, here is <payment> for what you've done so far."`
			choice
				`	"I'd be glad to take you back."`
				`	"I'll take you, but I'll notice that you haven't mentioned what we came so far for."`
			`	Eeeya smiles at her granddaughter. "Before I leave, I was just reminded about a present I have for you." She leads you and Anoo back to <ship>. "Captain, could you open the cargo hold and take us inside?" Anoo is astonished at the tons of acorn cookies waiting for her inside. "Thank you so much, grandma!"`
			choice
				`	"We should get going."`
			`	Eeeya turns to you and says, "You're right, we should. Thank you so much for reuniting us." She gives Anoo one last hug, and boards your ship.`
				accept

			label "really join"
			action
				set "acorn delights: eeeya joined"
			`	"No pun intended, of course," Anoo laughs. Eeeya chuckles, and says, "Captain, I still need to sell my factory in the normal Hai space before officially joining Unfettered."`
			choice
				`	"Sure thing. I'd be glad to take you back."`
					accept
				`	"Why did you change your mind so quickly?"`
			`	"Well, even without your input, I probably would have joined the Unfettered - as I said a while ago, my son's entire family died; Anoo's family is here. Anoo, I've got a surprise for you, still on the Captain's ship: 3 tons of acorn cookies! And <first>, would you take me back to <planet> so that I can sell off the factory?"`
				accept
	on visit
		dialog "You forgot Eeeya or some of her mountains of cookies. Maybe they're in one of your escorts?"

	on offer

After a long journey, we found her - she had gone to help the Unfettered as a trauma surgeon. Eeeya wasn't interested in joining the Unfettered, so brought her back home and received stocks for a one hundred credit salary for my efforts.`
			`Eeeya immediately gets off the ship and goes to check on the acorn cookie shop. A few minutes later, she is back with documents for you to sign.`
			`	"These will set you up with about a twentieth of the stock in my company; you'll receive the one hundred credits daily." As you sign the documents, she continues. "I'd just like to thank you again for helping me find Anoo. It means a lot to me." You check that the stocks have been transferred to your account, and Eeeya heads off.`
				decline
			label uhai
			`Eeeya heads off to sell Acorn Delights' shop while you check in with spaceport security. Soon, she is back. "Well, there was a Hai who paid thirty million credits for that small place. I wonder why he thought it was worth so much - I sure don't. But I won't argue with that nutty person." She seems much merrier now that Anoo has been found. "I'll be ready to go when you are."`

After a long journey, we found her - she had gone to help the Unfettered as a trauma surgeon. Brought Eeeya back to <origin> so she could sell the shop, but both Hai are together on <destination> now. Received a total of five hundred thousand credits for my efforts.`

	on visit
